1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bilal Student ("the App"), developed and published by developer OneCo (ONECO Technologies / Skoolific) on behalf of Bilal School, collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when enrolled students access their educational portal.

We are dedicated to safeguarding student privacy and maintaining strict confidentiality of student educational records in full compliance with global student data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver educational and school services to enrolled students:

  • Student Profile & Authentication: Student username, unique student ID, full name, enrolled class/section, and authentication tokens provided by Bilal School administration.
  • Academic & Attendance Records: Subject grades, term marks, report cards, class attendance status, and homework assignments.
  • Online Practice & Assessments: Student responses, test submissions, quiz scores, and academic progress indicators.
  • Device & Notification Data: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used strictly to deliver exam reminders, timetable announcements, and academic updates.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): Camera or storage access only when the student or school explicitly chooses to display a student profile picture.

3. How We Use the Information

All information collected is used exclusively for educational and school operational purposes:

  • To authenticate enrolled students securely into their student dashboard.
  • To display personal grades, report cards, attendance status, and subject schedules.
  • To facilitate online student assessments, test taking, and learning activities.
  • To deliver real-time notices regarding school events, deadlines, and urgent notices.
  • To ensure app security, data integrity, and prevent unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share student data with third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

Data is strictly accessible only to:

  • The authenticated student and their verified parents/guardians.
  • Authorized teachers and administrators of Bilal School.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications) bound by confidentiality and data processing agreements.

5. Security of Student Data

We employ rigorous industry-standard security measures to protect student information:

  • All data transmissions are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL).
  • Authentication tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored securely.
  • Access controls ensure students can only view their own academic records and class assignments.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Bilal Student serves students of primary and secondary education, protecting minors' privacy is paramount. Student accounts are provisioned exclusively through authorized school administrators with parental or school consent. We do not engage in behavioral profiling, tracking, or targeted advertising.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Student academic records are maintained in accordance with Bilal School educational record retention policies. Upon student graduation, transfer, or written request by a parent or school administration, accounts and personal data can be removed or deactivated.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements or legal compliance. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
