Privacy Policy for Bilal Parent

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bilal School and Skoolific ("we", "our", or "us") collect, use, and protect information when you use the Bilal Parent mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The Bilal Parent app is designed exclusively for parents and legal guardians of students enrolled in Bilal School. We collect and process the following information:

  • Account Credentials: Username, password, and authentication tokens to verify parent identity.
  • Student Academic & Attendance Data: Student names, grades, mark lists, evaluation records, and daily attendance status provided by the school administration.
  • Financial & Fee Records: Tuition payment status, invoice balances, and payment receipt history.
  • Device Information & Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens used strictly to deliver school announcements, absence alerts, and grade notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide parents real-time access to their children's academic performance, attendance, and fee status.
  • Send urgent school announcements, event notifications, and payment reminders.
  • Facilitate communication between parents and school administration.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade parent or student personal information to any third parties or advertisers. Data is strictly processed for Bilal School's educational operations.

4. Data Security

All communication between the Bilal Parent mobile app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS). Access to student data requires authenticated parent credentials.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain student records in accordance with institutional school guidelines and applicable education records laws. Parents can request account review or data deletion by contacting the school administration or emailing support.
