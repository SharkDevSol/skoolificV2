1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bilal Staff ("the App"), developed and published by developer OneCo (ONECO Technologies / Skoolific) on behalf of Bilal School, collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when school faculty, teachers, and administrators use the mobile application.

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of school personnel and student educational records. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide authorized school management functionality:

  • Staff Account Credentials: Staff username, full name, role (class teacher, subject teacher, admin), assigned class, and contact details (email or phone number) provided by the school administration.
  • Academic & Attendance Records: Student attendance records, evaluation marks, exam grades, and teacher notes created by authorized staff members during their official duties.
  • Device & Notification Data: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used strictly to deliver school notifications and attendance alerts.
  • Media & Photos (Optional): Camera and gallery access only when a user explicitly chooses to take or upload a profile photo, worksheet, lesson material, or document attachment.

3. How We Use the Information

The collected information is used solely for educational and school operational purposes:

  • To authenticate teachers and staff securely into their assigned school portal.
  • To record, manage, and synchronize student attendance and academic grading.
  • To deliver real-time administrative announcements, alerts, and timetable updates.
  • To maintain system security, audit logs, and technical stability.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties or advertising networks. Information is only shared under the following strict conditions:

  • With Bilal School Administration: Authorized school administrators have access to academic records, attendance logs, and staff entries in accordance with school policies.
  • Essential Service Infrastructure: Trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Firebase for push notifications and encrypted cloud hosting) solely to deliver core app functionality.
  • Legal Requirements: If mandated by applicable law, regulation, or legitimate government request.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data transmissions between the App and our backend servers are strictly encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Access controls require authenticated credentials, and sensitive tokens are kept protected.
  • Database access is restricted to authorized server instances.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

School staff members have the right to review their profile details or request account deletion.

If you wish to request deletion of your account or personal data, you may:

  • Contact your Bilal School principal or system administrator directly.
  • Submit a formal data deletion request by emailing support@skoolific.com with your username and school branch.

Upon verification, non-statutory personal data will be removed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bilal Staff app is designed exclusively for adult school staff, teachers, and administrators (aged 18 and older). It is not directed at children. Student academic and attendance data stored through the platform is managed strictly pursuant to authorized school institutional agreements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App following any changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
