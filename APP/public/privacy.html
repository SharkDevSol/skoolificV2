Privacy Policy for IQRA Staff

Last updated: October 15, 2026

IQRA Academy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed by the IQRA Staff mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The IQRA Staff application is designed specifically for authorized faculty and school staff of IQRA Academy. To provide its core services, the application accesses:

  • Account Information: Staff username, staff ID, and authentication tokens required to securely verify staff identity.
  • Academic & Attendance Records: Student attendance markings, classroom reports, and internal messages created during school operations.
  • Device Information: Device tokens for sending push notifications regarding school announcements and schedule changes.

2. How We Use Information

All data collected is used solely for educational and school operational purposes, including:

  • Facilitating classroom attendance recording and reporting.
  • Synchronizing academic data with the school management portal.
  • Enabling secure communication between school teachers and guardians.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third-party advertisers or commercial entities. All communications are securely encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.

4. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult school staff and teachers only. It is not directed to children under 13.
