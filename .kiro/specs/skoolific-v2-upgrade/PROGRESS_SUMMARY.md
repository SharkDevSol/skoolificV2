4. **Documentation as You Go:** Document components while building rather than as a separate phase.

### Risk Mitigation:

1. **Testing Debt:** Don't skip testing tasks - they catch issues early
2. **Accessibility:** Address accessibility during development, not after
3. **Performance:** Monitor bundle size as you add features
